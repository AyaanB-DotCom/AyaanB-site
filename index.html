A note from the archive

#whoami

I'm intrigued to learn about what happens underneath the technology that we use everyday.

Sometimes that means opening up unfamiliar hardware, examining firmware, or figuring out why a piece of software behaves the way it does.

This website is where I document those investigations, experiments, and the occasional rabbit hole.

What I do

  1. 01 /

    Taking things apart

    I want to see and understand what was never meant to be seen.

  2. 02 /

    Following the evidence

    From binaries to firmware and network traffic, I enjoy investigating unfamiliar systems and figuring out what they’re actually doing.

  3. 03 /

    Writing things down

    I want to document the process, including the interesting details, mistakes, unexpected behavior, and things I learn along the way.
