Notes / CodePath / 003

Educational security lab · CYB102 · Project 5

Splunk CTF

Capture-the-flag style Splunk searches: Netflix catalog questions, then a PathCode Inc malware investigation using uploaded-hash, login, and web-server logs.

Educational lab. Authorized CodePath Intermediate Cybersecurity CTF. Answers below are from the course Splunk environment (index=main / Netflix host, and index=pathcode). Not a live production investigation.

01

Overview

Unit 5’s project is a Capture-the-Flag Splunk activity. Instead of a fully guided lab, each challenge asks for a hypothesis and a search that proves it—closer to how SOC analysts work an investigation. Part 1 searches Netflix catalog data. Part 2 investigates an attacker who entered systems at the fictional PathCode Inc.

02

Goals

Search & analysis
Apply Splunk search, analysis, and visualization techniques to concrete questions.
Investigation habits
Practice forming a hypothesis and proving it with logs rather than following a fixed click path.
Dataset fluency
Gain experience searching and correlating events across different hosts and indexes in a competitive CTF format.

03

Part 1 · Searching the Netflix Data

Base context: index=main source=Netflix (searches below use host=Netflix as recorded in the submission).

  1. Challenge 1

    How many TV shows on Netflix are in the Docuseries genre?

    Answer 790

    index=main host=Netflix type="TV Show" listed_in="*Docuseries*"
| stats count

  2. Challenge 2

    How many movies on Netflix have a rating of TV-PG?

    Answer 1080

    index=main host=Netflix type="Movie" rating="TV-PG" | stats count

  3. Challenge 3

    How many movies on Netflix were released in the year 2020?

    Answer 1034

    index=main host=Netflix type="Movie" release_year=2020
| stats count

  4. Challenge 4

    What is the longest duration by season on Netflix, and what is its TV rating?

    Answer Grey’s Anatomy

    index=main host=Netflix type="TV Show"
| rex field=duration "(?P<seasons>\d+)"
| eval seasons=tonumber(seasons)
| sort -seasons
| table title, duration, rating
| head 1

  5. Challenge 5

    How many movies on Netflix are listed as rated PG-13 and listed in Action & Adventure?

    Answer 296

    index=main host=Netflix type="Movie" rating="PG-13" listed_in="*Action & Adventure*"
| stats count

  6. Challenge 6

    How many movies and TV shows on Netflix have their country of origin as Turkey?

    Answer 152

    index=main host=Netflix country="*Turkey*"
| stats count

  7. Challenge 7

    Which release year had the most movies rated G? (Not TV-G)

    Answer 2009

    index=main host=Netflix type="Movie" rating="G"
| stats count by release_year
| sort -count
| head 1

  8. Challenge 8

    What two TV-Y7 rated shows were released in 2019 and were added to Netflix on November 22, 2019?

    Answer Trolls: The Beat Goes On & The Dragon Prince

    index=main host=Netflix rating="TV-Y7" release_year=2019 date_added="November 22, 2019"
| table title

  9. Challenge 9

    Which year had the most TV Shows from the United States?

    Answer 2020

    index=main host=Netflix type="TV Show" country="*United States*"
| stats count by release_year
| sort -count
| head 1

  10. Challenge 10

    What is the oldest TV show by Release Year on Netflix?

    Answer Pioneers: First Women Filmmakers*

    index=main host=Netflix type="TV Show"
| sort release_year
| table title, release_year
| head 1

04

Part 2 · Investigating the Malware

Scenario: an attacker entered systems at PathCode Inc. Logs are under index=pathcode.

  1. Challenge 11

    What was the IP address that uploaded the malware (MD5 hash: 3AADBF7E527FC1A050E1C97FEA1CBA4D)?

    Answer 192.168.1.10

    host=uploadedhashes "File Hash"="3AADBF7E527FC1A050E1C97FEA1CBA4D"
| table *

  2. Challenge 12

    What usernames did that IP address try to login to the system as? Which one did they upload a file as?

    Answer ABurke

    host=failedlogins64 "192.168.1.10"
| table *

  3. Challenge 13

    What was the User Agent String of the attacker when they successfully uploaded a file?

    Answer Firefox/89.0

    host=webserver02 "192.168.1.10"
| table *

  4. Challenge 14

    Did any other users also upload a file around that time? If so, who and what was their IP address?

    Answer Yes — IP 192.168.1.8 uploaded Style.css at 17:49

    host=uploadedhashes
| table *

  5. Challenge 15

    Looking at the uploaded hashes, what were the files called that the two users uploaded? Which one seems like it was malicious?

    Answer Evilscript.exe and Style.css — Evilscript.exe was the malicious upload

    host=uploadedhashes
| table *
