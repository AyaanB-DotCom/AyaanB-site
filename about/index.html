About / purpose / method

About the archive.

AyaanB-DotCom is a pseudonymous, independent home for technical experiments, security research, and notes on computing.

Purpose

The archive is intended to hold work about the systems beneath everyday interfaces: embedded devices, software behavior, network boundaries, and the practical methods used to examine them.

It distinguishes incomplete notes from finished documents. Draft labels are deliberate; no research finding is implied where supporting material has not been filed.

Working method

Subjects are approached through observation, documentation, and small repeatable experiments. The goal is a durable research record, not a stream of promotional claims.

Privacy

The public identity used here is AyaanB-DotCom. The site does not publish biographical details, personal contact information, trackers, or analytics.

Archive format: static HTML, shared CSS, and optional vanilla JavaScript enhancements. Every document remains readable and navigable without scripts.
